## What you get

- A robot that can't be certified can't be sold. Compliance is where most hardware
  projects stall — a maze of directives, harmonized standards and documentation
  that nobody on the team trained for. OhhO Comply makes it a workflow.
- Comply maps your robot — pulled straight from its OhhO Build design and
  deployment profile — to the standards that actually apply. For industrial
  robots: the EU Machinery Regulation and CE marking, ISO 10218 / ISO 15066, ISO
  13849 functional safety, and regional electrical standards like UL and IEC
  60204. For AGVs and AMRs: ISO 3691-4, the warehouse robotics safety standard,
  plus ANSI/ITSDF B56.5 in the US. For service and personal care robots: ISO
  13482. For the North American market: ANSI/RIA R15.06, the US equivalent of ISO
  10218. And the foundational IEC 61508 functional safety standard underpins them
  all.
- For each requirement it tracks evidence, owners and status, generates the
  technical construction file and Declaration of Conformity, and keeps an
  immutable audit trail — so when an auditor or a customer asks, the proof is one
  click away.

## Features

- **Applicability engine** — Comply reads your robot's design and use case and
  tells you which directives and standards actually apply — industrial, AGV/AMR,
  service, medical, agricultural — no guessing.
- **AGV/AMR safety (ISO 3691-4)** — The warehouse robotics safety standard —
  covering driverless industrial trucks, speed control, safety zones, obstacle
  detection and emergency stops. Plus ANSI/ITSDF B56.5 for the US market.
- **Service robot safety (ISO 13482)** — Safety requirements for personal care
  robots and mobile servant robots — directly relevant to household and service
  robots heading to market.
- **US industrial safety (ANSI/RIA R15.06)** — The North American equivalent of
  ISO 10218 — industrial robot and robot system safety requirements for the US
  market, harmonized with the international standard.
- **Foundational functional safety (IEC 61508)** — The root functional safety
  standard that ISO 13849 and other domain standards derive from — referenced
  throughout the compliance chain.
- **Guided checklists** — Each standard becomes a tracked list of requirements,
  each with evidence, an owner and a status.
- **Risk assessment** — Built-in templates for ISO 12100 hazard analysis and ISO
  13849 performance-level determination.
- **Document generation** — Auto-assemble the technical construction file, risk
  assessment and Declaration of Conformity.
- **Audit-ready trail** — Every change is logged and timestamped, producing a
  defensible compliance history.
- **Stays in sync** — Change a part in OhhO Build and Comply flags the
  requirements that need re-review.

## How it works

1. **Import your robot** — Comply pulls the design, sensors and use case from
   OhhO Build.
2. **See what applies** — It maps the applicable directives and standards
   automatically.
3. **Work the checklist** — Assign owners, attach evidence, run the risk
   assessment.
4. **Generate the file** — Export the technical file, Declaration of Conformity
   and audit trail for certification.

## FAQ

**Does Comply certify my robot?**
Comply prepares everything a certification needs — the technical file, risk
assessment and evidence — and on Forge connects you with certification partners.
The certificate itself is issued by an accredited body, not OhhO.

**Which standards are covered?**
Industrial robot safety (CE / Machinery Regulation, ISO 10218, ISO 15066, ISO
13849, UL/IEC 60204), AGV/AMR safety (ISO 3691-4, ANSI/ITSDF B56.5), service
robot safety (ISO 13482), US industrial safety (ANSI/RIA R15.06), and
foundational functional safety (IEC 61508) — with custom standards available on
Forge.

**Does Comply cover warehouse AGV/AMR safety?**
Yes. ISO 3691-4 is the safety standard for driverless industrial trucks —
covering speed control, safety zones, obstacle detection and emergency stops.
Comply maps it to your AGV/AMR design automatically, plus ANSI/ITSDF B56.5 for
the US market.

**What about service and household robots?**
ISO 13482 covers personal care robots — mobile servants, physical assistant
robots and person carrier robots. If you're building a household or service
robot, Comply maps the requirements that apply to your category.
